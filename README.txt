OfferNest CPA website starter
=============================

Included:
- index.html: responsive Bengali landing page
- privacy.html: starter privacy policy
- disclosure.html: affiliate/CPA disclosure

Important:
1. This is a template, not a live hosted website and it does not include a domain.
2. Replace the demo offer links with your own approved CPA tracking URLs.
3. Do not use fake claims, spam, fake leads, or collect sensitive personal data.
4. Check the CPA network's age requirements, Bangladesh availability, traffic rules and payout methods. If you are under 18, ask a parent/guardian to help with accounts and payments.
5. To use a custom domain, register one through a reputable registrar and connect it to a hosting provider. Domain and hosting usually cost money; availability/prices vary.
